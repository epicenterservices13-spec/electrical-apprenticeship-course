## Exercise 3: Supervised practical task

**Task:** Build a single-resistor circuit on the trainer, predict current, then verify by measurement.

- **Setting:** Supervised bench, ≤ 12 V DC trainer
- **PPE:** Safety glasses
- **Controls:** Trainer OFF while wiring; instructor approves before power-on
- **Stop conditions:** Hot resistor, burning smell, unexpected reading

**Instructor approval to begin:** ____________________

1. With trainer OFF, measure the 1 kΩ resistor and record actual R.
2. Wire it across the supply. Set the supply to 10 V. Predict I = E ÷ R.
3. Move the red lead to the mA jack, put the meter **in series**, and get approval.
4. Power on, record current, power off. **Return the red lead to the V/Ω jack.**

| Measurement | Expected | Recorded | Unit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| R (de-energized) | 950–1,050 | ______ | Ω |
| Predicted I | ≈ 10 | ______ | mA |
| Measured I | within 5% of predicted | ______ | mA |
