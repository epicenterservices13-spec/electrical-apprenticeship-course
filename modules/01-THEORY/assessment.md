## Part A: Multiple choice

Circle one answer for each question.

1. The unit of electrical resistance is the:
   A. volt  B. ampere  C. ohm  D. watt
2. The symbol used for current in Ohm's law is:
   A. C  B. I  C. A  D. R
3. Voltage is best described as:
   A. the rate of flow of charge  B. the opposition to current  C. the potential difference between two points  D. the rate of energy use
4. If the voltage across a fixed resistor is doubled, the current will:
   A. halve  B. stay the same  C. double  D. quadruple
5. If the resistance in a circuit is doubled while voltage stays the same, the current will:
   A. halve  B. stay the same  C. double  D. quadruple
6. Which formula gives power from current and resistance?
   A. P = E ÷ I  B. P = I² × R  C. P = R ÷ I  D. P = E × R
7. ⚠ Before measuring resistance with a DMM, you must:
   A. set the meter to the A jack  B. make sure the circuit is energized  C. de-energize and isolate the component  D. touch both probe tips with your fingers
8. ⚠ After measuring current, the red test lead should be:
   A. left in the A or mA jack  B. returned to the V/Ω jack  C. removed from the meter permanently  D. connected to the COM jack
